La Nostra Storia

Old-World Craft Meets the Pacific Tide.

Nestled on Abbott Street just moments from the historic Ocean Beach Pier, Pazzo's Italian was born from a simple devotion: honoring time-tested Italian culinary traditions with the effortless, sun-drenched spirit of coastal San Diego.

Every morning, our kitchen extrudes bronze-die tagliatelle, folds delicate ricotta agnolotti by hand, and simmers San Marzano DOP tomatoes with cold-pressed Sicilian olive oil. As evening settles over the Pacific, local line-caught sea bass, Santa Barbara uni, and Baja prawns meet the warmth of oak-fired aromatics.
